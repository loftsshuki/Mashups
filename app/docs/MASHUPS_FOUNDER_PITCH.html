01The 30-second
elevator pitch

Creators break songs. Nobody built the rails.

Short-form creators drive music discovery, but the system behind it is fragmented. Creators struggle to find music they can confidently use. Artists and labels struggle to activate creators and measure what actually drives growth.

Mashups brings the loop together. Creators discover licensed tracks, generate platform-ready edits and mashups, publish with instant license proof and attribution, track performance, and earn from successful campaigns. Rights holders launch tracks into creator communities, reward performance, and spot breakout momentum before it is obvious elsewhere.
