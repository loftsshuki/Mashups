# Founding House Outreach Sprint

This sprint can run before production Supabase exists. Use `/outreach?demo=1` as a device-local working pipeline, export the CSV at the end of every session, and avoid shared devices.

## Ten-Day Objective

- Research 50 rights-reachable independent electronic prospects.
- Qualify 25 against authority, release timing, artist participation, and campaign fit.
- Send 20 personalized first touches.
- Generate 8 substantive replies.
- Book 5 fit reviews.
- Obtain 2 track packets for rights review.
- Select 1 founding pilot, contingent on production infrastructure and signed rights.

## Daily Sequence

1. Days 1-2: Build the first 25-label and manager list. Record the evidence behind every qualification.
2. Day 3: Add 15 dance-focused distributors and five scene venues with introduction leverage.
3. Day 4: Personalize and send the first ten emails. Do not send bulk copy.
4. Day 5: Send ten manager or founder DMs and request warm introductions from venues.
5. Day 6: Follow up on high-priority opens and replies with the one-page operating offer.
6. Day 7: Run the first fit reviews and disqualify unclear rights immediately.
7. Day 8: Request masters, authority evidence, release windows, and artist commitments from qualified teams.
8. Day 9: Build operator packets for the strongest two candidates.
9. Day 10: Select the primary and backup pilot; document every unresolved launch gate.

## Qualification Questions

- Who owns the master, and who can approve edits and creator whitelisting?
- Which territories are available for organic and paid use?
- Is the track unreleased, active, or catalog, and what decision must this test answer?
- Can the artist provide reactions, reposts, and one live creator session?
- Is there a clean master, instrumental, artwork, metadata, and split information?
- Will the team accept a matched holdout and a report that may show no lift?

## First Email

**Subject:** A controlled creator test for `{{track_title}}`

We run a 14-day, rights-bound test for independent electronic releases. Mashups contracts the creator opportunity floor, three hook tests, explicit attribution, and a matched-control evidence report. Creators are paid, campaign uses are licensed, and only proven posts become paid-media candidates. We guarantee the operating work, never streams or virality.

Would a 20-minute rights and campaign-fit review be useful for `{{track_title}}`?

## Follow-Up

The useful distinction is that this is not an influencer blast. The pilot is designed to answer whether a specific hook structure creates incremental listener intent for a defined creator culture and city cell. If it does not, the report says so. If it does, the rightsholder receives the reusable creative evidence and an option to license winning posts for paid use.

## Fit-Review Exit Criteria

- Master authority is identifiable and documentable.
- Campaign edits, organic use, whitelisting, and paid caps can be licensed.
- At least one target market has a fillable creator cohort.
- The artist accepts participation obligations.
- The campaign has a real decision and enough budget to compensate creators.
- The team accepts explicit exclusions for streams, revenue, charts, and virality.

Do not promise launch until `/readiness` has no required blocker and the Growth License is signed.
