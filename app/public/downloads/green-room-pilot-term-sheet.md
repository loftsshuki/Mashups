# Mashups Artist-Direct Pilot Term Sheet

Status: discussion draft for counsel review.

## Grant

Non-exclusive, revocable permission to ingest the approved master, analyze metadata, securely extract stems, store private processing assets, create platform-controlled cross-track derivatives with other approved tracks, stream creations inside Mashups, and produce 15- or 30-second watermarked video clips when separately enabled.

## Rights Required

The participant must control or document permission for 100% of the master and composition for the granted uses. Every sample, interpolation, featured performer, producer restriction, and co-writer approval must be disclosed.

## Prohibited Uses

No standalone audio or stem export, DSP distribution, paid advertising, sublicensing outside the platform, model training, synthetic voice use, political use, or use in restricted brand categories without a separate written amendment.

## Controls

The participant may define pairing allowlists or blocklists, pitch/tempo limits, territories, sensitive-use restrictions, and a takedown request. Mashups may quarantine any track for rights, safety, or quality concerns.

## Term And Withdrawal

Suggested pilot term: 90 days. New creation and export stop after withdrawal. Existing public clips follow the negotiated archive and takedown rules. Rights and audit records remain as required for dispute handling.

## Economics

No guaranteed advance, streams, chart position, or virality. Any challenge prize, subscription pool, or campaign fee requires a written schedule defining gross receipts, deductions, reporting, payment timing, and tax treatment.

## Data

Mashups may use product events and de-identified quality data to operate and improve the service. Source audio, stems, lyrics, or voice likeness may not train external generative models.

## Liability And Disputes

Final language must address warranties, indemnity, liability caps, governing law, emergency takedowns, audit rights, and conflicting claims. This draft is not a substitute for music counsel.
