## Company Context

Mashups is being developed as a rights-aware music growth platform.

Its intended operating loop is:

1. A rightsholder supplies a track and proves authority.
2. Mashups identifies promising hooks and creative structures.
3. Creators receive licensed campaign opportunities.
4. Creators publish short-form content using the track.
5. Mashups measures attributable behavior and incremental lift.
6. Winning content can be licensed for paid amplification.
7. Artists, labels, creators, and Mashups share in the resulting value.
8. Performance evidence improves future campaign selection and execution.

Mashups is not intended to be another passive royalty-free music library.

The larger thesis is that Mashups could become an operating layer connecting:

- Independent music supply
- Explicit usage rights
- Short-form creator distribution
- Creative testing
- Attribution
- Paid amplification
- Artist participation
- Creator compensation
- Campaign evidence
- A&R intelligence
- Repeatable release growth

## Current Pilot Concept

Initial proposed customer:

- Independent electronic artist or label
- Controls, or can quickly approve, the master recording
- Ideally controls or can clear the composition
- Has an active release or one arriving within approximately 90 days
- Can participate through reposting, reacting, commenting, or joining a creator activation
- Wants measurable testing rather than guaranteed virality

Initial proposed offer:

## Primary Strategic Question

Determine whether Mashups can build a defensible, profitable company by becoming the rights, testing, distribution, and measurement infrastructure for creator-led music growth.

Answer whether access to commercially meaningful "real music" is achievable without initially securing deals from the three major label groups.

Do not reduce the answer to "yes, use independent music." Determine exactly which music, which rightsholders, which contractual pathways, which economics, and which operating model could make this viable.
