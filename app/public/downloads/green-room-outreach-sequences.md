# Green Room Artist Outreach Sequences

## Direct Message

We are building Mashups, a closed Green Room where fans combine two artist-approved tracks and share a 15- or 30-second attributed video. No loose MP3, WAV, or stems. You control pairings, modifications, territory, and withdrawal. We are inviting a small group of one-stop electronic artists to test one track with us. Interested in seeing the pilot controls?

## First Email

Subject: Let fans remix one track without giving up the master

Mashups is testing a permissioned fan-creation format for independent electronic releases. Fans hear three structured versions from two approved tracks, keep one, and share a watermarked vertical clip. Every output carries both source passports. Standalone audio, stems, DSP distribution, paid advertising, and model training are disabled.

The artist chooses allowed pairings and can stop the pilot. We report actual creation, keep, share, retention, and rights-issue data rather than promising streams. We would like to test one one-stop track from your catalog.

## Follow-Up

The useful question is not whether a mashup gets one clever listen. It is whether fans repeatedly make, keep, and share sanctioned versions around a release. Our 90-day pilot is built to answer that while keeping rights and exports contained. I can send the Rights Passport and pilot term sheet.

## Discovery Call Questions

1. Who controls the master?
2. Who controls every composition share?
3. Are any samples, interpolations, or featured performances present?
4. Are stems available and may Mashups extract missing stems?
5. Which artists, genres, uses, territories, or modifications should be blocked?
6. Is 15/30-second watermarked video export acceptable?
7. What evidence would justify a larger challenge?
