# Mashups Green Room Artist Pilot Kit

## The Opportunity

Mashups lets fans make controlled, attributed combinations from artist-approved tracks. The pilot is designed to test whether fans repeatedly listen to, share, and fork sanctioned versions without releasing loose masters or stems.

## What The Artist Controls

- Which tracks participate.
- Which other opted-in tracks may pair with them.
- Maximum pitch and tempo changes.
- Explicit-content and sensitive-use restrictions.
- Territory and term.
- Whether 15- and 30-second watermarked video export is allowed.
- Immediate pause or withdrawal.

## What Mashups Does Not Permit

- MP3 or WAV export.
- Stem download.
- DSP distribution.
- Paid advertising without a separate written grant.
- Political, defamatory, illegal, or deceptive use.
- Generative-model training or voice cloning.
- Guaranteed streams, charts, revenue, or virality.

## Pilot Workflow

1. Complete the Rights Passport and pairing controls.
2. Upload one private master.
3. Mashups validates the file, analyzes structure, and quarantines unresolved rights or quality issues.
4. Two independent listeners review generated combinations.
5. Approved tracks enter a closed beta challenge.
6. The artist receives creation, keep, share, retention, and issue reporting.

## Pilot Evidence

- Pairing attempts and preflight rejection reasons.
- Render completion and candidate keep rate.
- Watermarked video share starts.
- Day-30 creator retention.
- Rights claims, muting, and takedown response time.
