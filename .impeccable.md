## Design Context

### Users

Mashups serves fans without production experience and DJs/producers with existing Logic or Ableton setups. The listening bench is for comparing audio on a phone or computer and recording a preference without seeing the processing method.

### Brand Personality

Follow the project's stated music-native, creative, community-driven tone. Keep instructions direct and approachable. These principles reflect the existing project instructions and creator discussion, not a new brand identity.

### Aesthetic Direction

For the listening bench, use a restrained, light studio worksheet with strong typography, warm neutrals, and one clear playback accent. DJ.Studio and Ableton are functional audio references; no request has been made to copy their visual designs. The broader brand palette remains undecided.

### Design Principles

- Make listening the primary action; show measurements after a preference is recorded.
- Offer the same listening functions on phones and desktops.
- Keep source identities out of blind comparisons.
- Distinguish measured signal properties, test fixtures, and human judgments.
- Use keyboard-accessible controls, readable contrast, and minimal motion.
